# Queryless Fact age penalty

## Frozen decision rule

Before scoring, require at least one additional valid durable Fact in a returned page, zero newly promoted stale time-sensitive Facts, and unchanged queried results and project scope. The synthetic examples and their validity and durability labels were fixed in `eval/runner/fact-age.ts` before scoring. No holdout was used. Page membership is a retrieval proxy, not evidence of an Agent action or task outcome.

## Method

Baseline revision `39d2cccd`; five paired hand-authored synthetic cases with identical in-memory Facts and Session scope, fixed clock `2026-11-23T00:00:00Z`, and limits of one or two. Both arms call real `recallSemanticFacts`. The treatment builds an isolated esbuild bundle with only the queryless confidence age divisor removed; queried scoring, near-twin capping and project filtering remain unchanged. Both arms leave stored Facts and their Decay fields unchanged. Queried control compares complete returned rows and scores, not just IDs. These cases do not assess time, tokens, production distributions, or unseen stale claims.

## Results

| Case | Baseline page | Treatment page | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Durable, limit 1 | fresh | durable | Membership change adds one valid durable Fact and displaces one valid time-sensitive Fact. |
| Same Facts, limit 2 | fresh, durable | durable, fresh | Reordering only; membership is unchanged. |
| Stale near-twin, limit 1 | current | current | Counterexample: the newer near-twin cap prevents promotion of the stale Fact. |
| Queried control, limit 2 | durable, fresh | durable, fresh | Complete returned results, including scores, unchanged. |
| Foreign-project control, limit 1 | local | local | No foreign Fact returned. |

The frozen criterion is met on this constructed cohort: one additional valid durable Fact in a page, zero newly promoted stale Facts, and unchanged controls. The gain at limit 1 is a membership change; at limit 2 it is only reordering. The stale case tests a near-twin, not unrelated stale Facts; it cannot establish that stale promotion is generally avoided.

**Verdict: inconclusive.** The fixtures demonstrate a possible tradeoff but are too few and deliberately constructed to establish the distribution of durable gains or stale regressions in real Recall. Retry with independently labeled real queryless Facts across projects, including stale non-near-twins and page-size variation, measured with matched scope and queried controls. No production change is proposed from this result alone.

Reproduce with `npx tsx eval/runner/fact-age.ts`; `npx vitest run test/eval-fact-age.test.ts` checks the paired outputs.
