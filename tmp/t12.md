## What to build

The project's own documentation stops describing this fork as built on the removed runtime. Right now the architecture section states it as the foundational fact, the code-pattern examples are written against it, the testing section describes mocking it, and the statistics block counts its functions. After #10 all of that is false.

Rewrite it to describe the single in-process Engine as it actually is — do not annotate the old text or leave it standing with a caveat. Remove the pointer in the domain-docs section that says the architecture section is knowingly stale, since it no longer will be.
